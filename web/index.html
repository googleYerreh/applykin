Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Checking, designs, PDF downloads, job search, tailoring, letters and the tracker are all free, with no limits. The optional AI polish uses your own key or a free model on your computer.

How is the resume score calculated?

It's a checklist of rules drawn from how tracking software reads files and from studies of how recruiters skim resumes. There is no official "resume score" anywhere, so Applykin shows every rule, its weight, and how strong the evidence behind it is.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, and that's on purpose. Applykin prepares a tailored resume, letter and answers, then opens the employer's page so you can review them and submit. Bots that apply for you tend to send weak applications and get blocked.

Where do the jobs come from?

From public job boards that companies publish themselves (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), plus the Arbeitnow and Jobicy feeds. The list refreshes every 6 hours.

What does a match score of 60 mean?

It's a good match: 75+ is strong, 60–74 good, 45–59 worth a look. The score shows how well your resume lines up with the ad, not your odds of being hired.
